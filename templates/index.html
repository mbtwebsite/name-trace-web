Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about creating and printing worksheets.

Are the worksheet generators free?

Yes. The worksheet generators are free to use, and you do not need to create an account.

Do the generators create printable PDF worksheets?

Yes. After you choose the worksheet options, the generator creates a PDF that you can preview, download, and print.

Can teachers use these worksheets in the classroom?

Yes. Teachers can use the generated worksheets for classroom instruction, centers, intervention, homework, tutoring, and other educational practice.

Can parents and homeschool families use the generators?

Yes. The tools are designed to be simple for families as well as teachers. Choose a skill, create the worksheet, and print it for home practice.

Can I make more than one version of a worksheet?

Yes. You can generate another worksheet whenever students need additional practice or a different set of problems.

Are answer keys included?

Some generators include answer pages or worked solutions. The available options are explained on each generator page.
